Our craft

Made the slow way, on purpose.

Every Vayan shirt starts as a single bolt of fabric, cut by hand to one tailor's own pattern. No factory line, no batch of a thousand identical pieces — just one shirt, then the next.

It takes longer. It also means the collar sits right, the stitching holds after the fiftieth wash, and no two runs are ever quite the same.
